## Literatur
- [Patent, 1995](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/07/ec/58/d551c802add507/US5437633.pdf): A method of treating a subject in cardiac arrest, comprises blocking the descending aorta of the subject and then perfusing the aortic arch of the subject with a protective solution. The protective solution is one capable of delivering oxygen to the heart of said subject, such-as a perfluorochemical blood substitute. Following perfusion, spontaneous circulation is restored. In an improved embodiment of the foregoing, a vasoconstrictor such as epinephrine is administered concurrently with the perfusing step in an amount effective to enhance coronary perfusion with said protective solution.
