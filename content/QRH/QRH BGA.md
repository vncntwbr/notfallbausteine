1. **Umrechnen V→A:** pH +0.03, pCO2 –5 mmHg
2. **Azidose/Alkalose:** HCO3- 22-26 mM, pCO2 35-45 mmHg, SBE proport. zu Met.
3. **Adäquate Kompensation?**
	- *Met. Azidose:* ∆pCO2 = SBE, max. bis 10 mmHg
	- *Met. Alkalose:* ∆pCO2 = SBE * 0.5
	- *Akut Resp. (<2-5 d):* SBE = 0
	- *Chron. Resp.:* SBE = ∆pCO2 * 0.33
4. **AG** = [Na+] – [Cl-] – [act. HCO3-], nw 8±4 mmol/l
	- *∆AG* = AG – nw AG = patholog. Anionen
	- *∆∆* = ∆AG + [HCO3-], nw 24±2 mmol/l (sonst zusätzliche Störung)
5. **Oxy:** 100% SpO2 ≈ 150 mmHg art. PaO2 | 95% ≈ 80 | 90% ≈ 60 | 85% ≈ 50
