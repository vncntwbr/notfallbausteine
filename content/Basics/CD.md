  - Cluster of Differentiation

- CD34+ = Blast
- CD3+ = T-cell 
- CD19+ = B-cell 
- CD33+ = Myeloid 
- CD34+ = Acute leukemia 
	- CD34+ CD19+ = B-ALL 
	- CD34+ CD3+ = T-ALL 
	- CD34+ CD33+ = AML 
	- CD34- CD33+ = APL (promyelocyte differentiated one step above blast)
