## Literatur
- System- & Patient-Benefits
	- POCUS-guided resuscitation probably reduces 28-day mortality (relative risk [RR] 0.88; 95% CI, 0.78-0.99), the duration of vasoactive medication (mean difference -0.73 d; 95% CI, -1.16 to -0.30), and the need for renal replacement therapy (RRT) (RR 0.80; 95% CI, 0.63-1.02) (low to moderate certainty evidence), and lactate clearance (high certainty evidence)[^1]

[^1]: Basmaji J. The Impact of Point-of-Care Ultrasound-Guided Resuscitation on Clinical Outcomes in Patients With Shock: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Crit Care Med. 2024 Nov 1;52(11):1661-1673. doi: 10.1097/CCM.0000000000006399. Epub 2024 Sep 18. PMID: 39298556.
