## Definition
- "The strategic decision to delay the initiation of fluid resuscitation and limit the volume of resuscitation fluids/blood products administered to the bleeding trauma patient by targeting a lower than normal blood pressure, usually a systolic blood pressure of 80–90 mmHg or a mean arterial pressure (MAP) of 50 mmHg" -[Kaafarani et al, 2014](https://sjs.sagepub.com/content/early/2014/04/28/1457496914524388.full)
