title: Sauerstoff (O2)
---

- **O2-Gabe zu FiO2**
	- 2 L Nasenbrille ≈ FiO2 30%
	- 6 L NRB Maske ≈ FiO2 60%
	- 10-15 L NRB Maske ≈ FiO2 90%
	- 60 L HFNC ≈ FiO2 100%
	- Maske ohne Reservoir max. 60%, cave CO2-Rückatmung <5 L
- **Target:** SpO2 92-96%, bei COPD/Asthma/Adipositas SpO2 88-92%[^1]

[^1]: Allardet-Servent. La Revue de Médecine Interne 2019. [Benefits and risks of oxygen therapy during acute medical illness: Just a matter of dose!](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0248866319304370)
